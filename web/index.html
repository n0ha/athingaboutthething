Chapter 1 · What this film isKapitola 1 · Čo je to za film

It is not a dogNie je to pes

A ship crashes into Antarctica. Thousands of years later, in the winter of 1982, a Norwegian helicopter chases a husky across the ice towards a small American research station. The Norwegians are shooting at the dog. The Americans take the dog in.Do Antarktídy padne loď. O tisíce rokov neskôr, v zime 1982, nórsky vrtuľník prenasleduje po ľade huskyho k malej americkej výskumnej stanici. Nóri po psovi strieľajú. Američania ho vezmú dnu.

What came with the dog is an organism that absorbs living things and copies them perfectly: body, voice, memory, habits. Twelve men are snowed in with it, the radio is dead, and any one of them may already be a copy. The film is about what that does to them.S psom prišiel organizmus, ktorý pohlcuje živé tvory a dokonale ich kopíruje: telo, hlas, pamäť aj zvyky. Dvanásť mužov je s ním uväznených v snehu, rádio mlčí a ktokoľvek z nich už môže byť kópiou. Film je o tom, čo to s nimi urobí.

    The film itself sets this up. The Bennings-Thing runs outside in someone else's parka while Bennings' vest lies shredded.     We fell for this one tooAj my sme sa nachytali

    Our first pass called it a different, khaki coat. Then we checked how frost looks in this film. When MacReady comes in from the storm, his dark leather jacket turns pale grey under frost. Childs has frost on his eyebrows and moustache, and his coat has the same speckled texture. Firelight is orange, so white frost reads as khaki. Rob Ager, who first proposed the coat theory in 2011, reached the same conclusion in 2017, and co-producer Stuart Cohen calls it “the same coat”.Prvý priechod to vyhodnotil ako inú, khaki bundu. Rob Ager, ktorý teóriu o bunde prišiel v roku 2011, dospel v roku 2017 k tomu istému a koproducent Stuart Cohen hovorí o „tej istej bunde“.

    The other readingDruhý výklad

    Or he was framedAlebo ho niekto očiernil

    The plot offers a frame-up and says so out loud. The light in MacReady's shack was on, though he had switched it off. His belongings had been moved to the storeroom, where anyone could take them. In the 1981 screenplay he studies the long johns like a detective (“Size large”) and the furnace garment is the Thing setting him up.Dej ponúka očiernenie a hovorí to nahlas. V MacReadyho chatrči svietilo, hoci svetlo zhasol. Jeho veci presunuli do skladu, kde si ich mohol vziať ktokoľvek. V scenári z roku 1981 skúma spodky ako detektív („Size large“) a odev v peci je Vec, ktorá ho chce očierniť.

    And MacReady spends the whole film destroying Things, ending with the station itself. The film never shows two Things helping each other, so that is a pattern, not proof.A MacReady celý film Veci ničí, nakoniec aj celú stanicu. Film nikde neukáže, že by si dve Veci pomáhali, takže je to vzorec, nie dôkaz.

    Our verdictNáš verdikt

    The film does not prove who the Thing is, and it seems cut to keep it that way: the two survivors are exactly the two men whose blood tests you cannot check. For Childs, “human” and “Thing” stay level. Everything against him is opportunity. MacReady is open. If the film hints at anyone, it hints at him.Film nedokazuje, kto je Vec, a zdá sa, že ho tak aj zostrihali: dvaja preživší sú presne tí dvaja, ktorých krvný test sa nedá overiť. Pri Childsovi ostávajú „človek“ a „Vec“ vyrovnané. Všetko proti nemu je len príležitosť. MacReady je otvorený. Ak film niekoho naznačuje, tak jeho.

    If Carpenter's “giant hint in the middle of the film” is real, our best candidate is the trail of torn clothes from 00:41 to 01:14, read through Fuchs' rule. It is a candidate, not an answer. Carpenter has never named the clue, and the plot itself explains the trail as a frame-up. The two men are still waiting by the fire, and so are we.Ak Carpenterova „obrovská nápoveda v strede filmu“ existuje, naším najlepším kandidátom je stopa roztrhaného oblečenia od 00:41 po 01:14, čítaná cez Fuchsovo pravidlo. Je to kandidát, nie odpoveď. Carpenter indíciu nikdy nepomenoval a dej sám stopu vysvetľuje ako očiernenie. Dvaja muži stále čakajú pri ohni a my s nimi.

    We analysed the theatrical cut from a Blu-ray source (1920×816, 23.976 fps). Five independent passes logged the film scene by scene: who is present, who is alone, props, costumes, breath and eyes. The final scene was analysed frame by frame. Face detection and recognition ran on every frame to measure screen time and catchlights. Only after that did we read what the filmmakers and fans have said, and then we re-tested our own conclusions. One of them did not survive. Finally, two rival AI models attacked the verdict. They found two logical mistakes and a trail we had scored piece by piece and never joined. We checked their claims against the frames and the 1981 screenplay and changed the verdict.Analyzovali sme kinovú verziu z Blu-ray (1920×816, 23,976 fps).
