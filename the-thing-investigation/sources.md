1. Nezávislé pozorovania (dych, oči, fľaša, náušnica) sa zhodujú s overenými vyjadreniami štábu.
2. Pri bunde ma externý zdroj upozornil na chybu v mojom farebnom argumente. Výsledkom je oprava,
   nie prevzatie záveru: nové testy obrazu (námraza na MacReadym, textúra, miesto prešitia) som urobil sám.

   filmu, ktoré analýza zaznamenala: Bennings-Vec dýcha viditeľne (00:48:00.9, vyvracia dychový test);
   Bennings-Vec v cudzej parke (00:47:55, pravidlo o odeve); Blairove poznámky „It needs to be alone…“
   (00:45:56); hudobný „tlkot“ pri psovi osamote (00:14:42). Žiadny z nich sám osebe nerozhoduje.

## 1. John Carpenter — indícia, zámerná nejednoznačnosť, kto je Vec

### 1.1 „Je tam veľká indícia“ — Academy Museum Gala, 17. 10. 2026 [S01]
- **Kto/kde:** John Carpenter, krátky rozhovor na červenom koberci Academy Museum Gala (LA) s Reece Feldmanom; 61-sekundové video zverejnila The Academy na X 18. 10. 2026 („John Carpenter tells Reece Feldman your THE THING theories are wrong at the Academy Museum gala.“).
- **Presné znenie** (00:22–00:39 klipu; otázka: „what do you think is the worst theory someone has given you about the ending of that movie?“):
  > CARPENTER: "Every idea that people have is not the right one. I know the right one."
  > FELDMAN: "Has no one guessed it?"
  > CARPENTER: "It's right in the movie too. There is a big clue. No one has guessed it."
- **Overenie:** **overené v origináli** — video stiahnuté priamo z tweetu @TheAcademy (id 2111861208768819334, cez syndikačné API X), prepis strojovo (Whisper small.en); odporúčam ešte raz vypočuť 00:27–00:40. SYFY Wire (Josh Weiss, 30. 10. 2026) to cituje mierne zlúčene („It's right in the movie, too. There is a big clue and no one has guessed it.“) — skrátenie médiom, nie Carpenterovo znenie.
- **Kontext/opatrnosť:** Carpenter neuvádza, čo je indícia, ani kto je Vec. Ide o 15-sekundovú odpoveď na červenom koberci; Carpenter je pri tejto téme dlhodobo žartovný (šek poštou, „sworn to secrecy“ — pozri 1.6). Nie je to prvé takéto vyjadrenie: už v októbri 2023 (Colbert, S12) povedal, že odpoveď sa dá zistiť pozorným sledovaním („Yes, you can.“), a 22. 3. 2025 (Q&A s Bong Joon Ho, S13) hovoril o „a giant hint in the middle of the film“. Výrok z 2026 to len zopakoval („no one has guessed it“).

### 1.5 Audiokomentár Carpenter + Russell (Universal Collector's Edition DVD, 1998) [S09]

Časy nižšie sú časy tejto nahrávky ≈ časy filmu. **Priradenie hovoriaceho** je odvodené z obsahu a výšky hlasu (Carpenter ~100–115 Hz, Russell ~150+ Hz) — pri výmene 1:40:42–1:40:54 je neisté, odporúčam vypočuť.

### 1.6 Ďalšie Carpenterove výroky (chronologicky)
- **Creative Screenwriting (Erik Bauer), pôvodne roč. 6, č. 1 (1999); web 22. 7. 2014** [S10] — **overené v origináli (text rozhovoru)**. Na otázku, či Lancaster opustil Campbellov šťastný koniec:
  > "His original ending had both MacReady and Charles [sic] turning into the Thing and being rescued in the spring. The helicopter lands and out they come out, 'Hey, which way to a hot meal?' I thought no, let's not do that. It was a little too glib."
  - Pozor: **rozpor so Stuartom Cohenom** (sekcia 4), podľa ktorého je výsledný koniec „little changed from Bill Lancaster's first draft“ a Lancaster považoval oboch za ľudí. Lancasterove návrhy (prvý draft) som nevidel; verejne dostupný je „Second Draft, March 4, 1981“ (sekcia 4.3), ktorý sa s filmom zhoduje a nič nerozhoduje.
- **Reddit 2013 (fáma, tretia ruka)** [S11] — Redditor *kleinbl00* (cez Giant Freakin Robot → io9/Gizmodo 19. 2. 2013, CinemaBlend 20. 2. 2013) tvrdil, že jeho kamarát počul od Carpentera: *"He said he never understood where all the confusion came from. The last frame of The Thing is Kurt Russell and Keith David staring each other down, harshly backlit. It's completely, glaringly obvious that Kurt Russell is breathing and Keith David is not."* → **neoverené (len citované ďalej)**; io9 to samo označilo „completely unverified rumor“. Stuart Cohen neskôr (Outpost #31 Q&A) uvádza, že Carpenter vyhlásil „all the rumors are untrue“ — pôvodný Carpenterov výrok som nedohľadal.
- **The Late Show with Stephen Colbert (CBS), segment zverejnený na YouTube 27. 10. 2023 („John Carpenter: We Wouldn't Stand a Chance Against 'The Thing'“, id fShQU7HWcwQ), 01:43–02:47** [S12] — **overené v origináli (video, strojový prepis)**:
  > COLBERT: "... is there an actual answer to the question, is Childs the Thing?"
  > CARPENTER: "There's an absolute answer to that question. I do know the answer to that question."
  > COLBERT: "Don't tell me any more. ... Can I figure out that answer merely by watching the film?"
  > CARPENTER: "If you watch it really carefully and you send me a check in the mail, I will tell you."
  > COLBERT: "No, I don't want to know. I just want to know whether I can figure it."
  > CARPENTER: "Yes, you can."
  - Toto je **najstaršie dohľadané Carpenterovo tvrdenie, že odpoveď sa dá vyčítať z filmu** (október 2023, teda pred výrokmi 2025 a 2026). Kontext je žartovný (šek poštou), ale „Yes, you can.“ je jednoznačné. Pozor: otázka je formulovaná konkrétne na Childsa („is Childs the Thing?“), Carpenter však neprezrádza smer.
- **Academy Museum, David Geffen Theater, 22. 3. 2025 — Q&A po 4K projekcii, moderoval Bong Joon Ho** [S13] — **overené v origináli (video, strojový prepis small.en + medium.en)**: YouTube „2025.03.22 THE THING Q&A with John Carpenter, moderated by Bong Joon Ho (Academy Museum)“, nahral Edward Wang (súkromný záznam z publika, zverejnené 18. 2. 2026), id ONOecgYyHkI, čas 21:25–22:47:
  > BONG: "Can I ask you a question that you're probably really sick of? ... So who is the thing?"
  > CARPENTER: "Well, there's a giant hint in the middle of the film. But I can tell you who was the thing, but you need to send an amount of money in an envelope to my house. And then I consider, I can't tell you that. I can't tell you. I've been sworn to secrecy."
  > BONG: "But the actors knew, right?"
  > CARPENTER: "No. They had no clue. But they had to play a human. You see? The creature imitates perfectly. He could be one of us. He could be somebody in the audience. And there's no way of telling. So I knew. They didn't know. Actors don't know anything."
  - Fanúšik Joe Russo (@joerussotweets, 23. 3. 2025) to zhrnul ako „a hint in the middle of THE THING that reveals whether MacReady or Childs is The Thing by the end of the movie“ a „Carpenter said both actors don't know...“ (texty tweetov overené cez X syndication API). GamesRadar (24. 3. 2025) a GeekTyrant prevzali Russov výklad (fľaša = „Thing has won“) — to je **fanúšikova interpretácia**, nie Carpenterova.

## 2. Dean Cundey — svetlo v očiach, svietenie záveru

### 2.1 Pôvod tvrdenia o „eye light“: Cundeyho komentár na Blu-ray Scream Factory (2016) [S05]
- **Médium:** nový audiokomentár s kameramanom Deanom Cundeym, moderoval Rob Galluzzo (Blumhouse, Killer POV), na 2-diskovom *Collector's Edition* Blu-ray od Scream Factory (vydanie ohlásené na 11. 10. 2016). Prvá publikácia citátov: Rob Galluzzo, „Did Dean Cundey Just Reveal The Biggest Secret Of THE THING?“, Blumhouse.com, 29. 8. 2016.
- **Cundey o krvnom teste (podľa Galluzzovho prepisu komentára):**
  > "So we were looking for some kind of a subtle way, to say which one of these (men) might be human. You'll notice there's always an eye light, we call it, a little gleam in the eye of the actor. It gives life."
  > "But not here. Not there." / "There is right here." / "And there is right here."
  > "But there is no eye light (on Palmer). Let's make it look subtle like he's different and the audience won't know until later. So he has dead eyes."
- **Cundey o ZÁVERE (ten istý komentár):**
  > "That was discussed. Should one of these guys be treated as if one of them are 'the thing?' But no, John very deliberately wanted to leave the question of if one, or both, or neither of these guys is 'the thing.'"
- **Kto rozšíril teóriu na záver:** nie Cundey, ale moderátor Galluzzo v tom istom článku: *"I'll just say it. In those final shots. Kurt Russell has the gleam lighting in his eyes. Childs does not! Make of that what you will."*
- **Overenie:** **overené v origináli** pre text Blumhouse (archivovaná pôvodná stránka, Wayback 2017-10-23, prečítaná celá). Pasáž o krvnom teste z disku som nepočul → **overené zo sekundárneho zdroja** (prepis moderátora). Pasáž o závere je **overená v origináli** (audio výňatok S28, nižšie). Odporúčanie: pasáž o krvnom teste overiť na disku Scream Factory CE (2016).
- **Originálne audio komentára (výňatok) — overené v origináli** [S28]: Rob Galluzzo zverejnil 15. 10. 2024 na YouTube výňatok z komentára („PROOF That Dean Cundey NEVER Gave Away The Ending To The #Thing“, id icOSKO7jBDs, 71 s; strojový prepis):
  > GALLUZZO: "Now, if we really want to analyze based on what we've learned earlier in this commentary, I see a gleam in Kurt Russell's eye. I'm just saying, we haven't gotten a good look at Childs yet."
  > CUNDEY: "No, and that was, you know, really sort of discussed with, you know, should one of these guys be treated as if they are, so that the audience afterwards can say, oh, yes, I see. But, no, it was, you know, John very deliberately wanted to leave the question as to whether, you know, one or both of these or neither of these guys were the thing."
  > GALLUZZO: "I'm just going to say, Dean, I see the gleam in one of these guys' eyes. Not the other. Just saying."
  > CUNDEY: "Yes. Yes, indeed. We won't say which." (žartovne)
  - **Galluzzo v popise videa:** "Earlier in this commentary, Dean reveals that for the 'blood test' scene (ONLY!), they purposely lit the eyes of the people that were human ... I jokingly implied in the commentary that maybe they did the same for the final scene ... and he goes along with the joke, but debunks my suggestion. FOR YEARS, people have written about how he revealed the ending, when it was, in fact, just me being silly. Apologies to Dean!!!!"
  - → **Pôvodca rozšírenia teórie na záver ju sám v roku 2024 označil za vtip.** Cundeyho „Yes, indeed. We won't say which.“ je pristúpenie na žart hneď po vete, že Carpenter chcel otázku nechať otvorenú.
- **Šírenie:** io9/Gizmodo (2016), CinemaBlend (Dirk Libbey, 30. 8. 2016), Mental Floss (Jay Serafino, 7. 9. 2016), JoBlo, Uproxx — tie z toho urobili titulky typu „záhada môže byť vyriešená“; väčšina pritom vynecháva Cundeyho vetu, že pri závere sa to zámerne NEurobilo.

### 2.2 Cundey v SYFY Wire (2022) [S03]
- **Krvný test:**
  > "I took a certain liberty that when the guy we're most suspicious of is seen, I didn't put the eye light in his eyes. I didn't put that little sparkle that we use most of the time on characters to create the sense of life, of intelligence. I kept the light out of his eyes, so his eyes were dark and dead. I think, just subconsciously, the audience sensed that. It wasn't until years later that I said, 'Okay, well, let me tell you what I did.'"
- **Záver:**
  > "John never would reveal the answer or maybe never had an answer. He just wanted to leave it hanging, so it would be an intriguing ending. We came up with various endings as we were working. [One such] ending [had] Kurt [as] the only one left, a helicopter comes in ... Or should the two of them be rescued and then we find out that the other character is really the Thing ... There were all kinds of stories written, mostly by the crew, as we sat shivering."
- **Overenie:** **overené v origináli** (vlastný rozhovor SYFY Wire).
- **Ďalší štáb k tomu istému (SYFY Wire 2022):** prvý asistent kamery Clyde Bryan: *"Everybody has an eye-light, except David [Clennon]. And it was discussed at the time ..."* a o závere: *"There's huge rumors and stuff about the ending of the movie. Having been there, I'd have to agree with John that most of that's bunk. ... The only time I remember that there was a lightning clue that someone was the Thing, was in the scene where they're all tied up ... But on the ending, I don't remember any conversations about the light in people's eyes."*

### 2.3 Carpenterova reakcia (2023) — pozri 1.2 [S02]
Carpenter: "He has no clue ... He puts the lights up, and we were in the snow." Pozor: Carpenter tu reaguje na teóriu *o závere* (Childs bez odlesku), nie na Cundeyho tvrdenie o krvnom teste.

### 2.3b Cundey o svetle exteriérov a ohňa — American Cinematographer (Jim Hemphill), „Flashback: The Thing“, theasc.com, 2017 [S27]
- **Overené v origináli** (text rozhovoru):
  > "I tried to create a strong sense of the cold by creating some contrast — the interiors were often lit with a warmer light so the audience would notice the difference between that and the exteriors, which we kept in blue tones, particularly at night."
  > "I found these very intense blue lights used on airport runways ... The snow would reflect any color we put on it, so that made the outside even bluer and cooler."
  > "We tried to use the flamethrowers and the flares the characters carry as sources of light, in some sense allowing the actors to do their own lighting."
  > O storyboardoch: "I would say our approach was somewhere in between having storyboards and just making it up as you go. We had some excellent storyboards by Mike Ploog ..."
- V rozhovore nie je zmienka o „eye light“ ani o záverečnej scéne zvlášť. Pre analýzu záveru: nočný exteriér = modré tóny + oheň ako (čiastočne) praktický zdroj; odlesky v očiach v takej scéne závisia od polohy voči ohňu, čo zodpovedá Carpenterovej poznámke „we were in the snow“.

### 2.4 Záver k otázke „pochádza tvrdenie od Cundeyho?“
- **Áno, ale len pre scénu krvného testu** (Palmer bez odlesku). To Cundey tvrdí sám, opakovane (2016, 2022), a podporuje to Clyde Bryan.
- **Pre záverečnú scénu Cundey zámer výslovne poprel** (2016: „John very deliberately wanted to leave the question…“; 2022: „John never would reveal the answer or maybe never had an answer“). Rozšírenie na Childsa pochádza od Roba Galluzza (Blumhouse 2016) a od médií/fanúšikov.
- Konkrétne vyjadrenie Cundeyho o svietení záverečnej scény (ohne, exteriér v Stewart, B.C.) som nenašiel, okrem toho, že v snehu „he puts the lights up“ (Carpenter). Pozri aj komentár 1998 (Carpenter: „as the light is kind of getting darker and darker on you…“), sekcia 1.5.

## 3. Herci — Keith David (Childs), Kurt Russell (MacReady)

### 3.1 Keith David
- **The Guardian, 25. 3. 2024 („How we made…“, interviews by Simon Bland)** [S04] — **overené v origináli**:
  > "I hear lots of theories about the final sequence. We played it various ways; as if I was the Thing, as if it was MacReady, and as if it was neither of us. People wonder why there's no breath coming out of my mouth in the cold after the station burns down, and say it had to be me. But I say that if I'm downstage of the fire you wouldn't see steam coming from my mouth because there's too much heat. That's how I explain it, but it's your movie, your experience. The Thing is whoever you think it is."
  - Dôležité pre forenznú analýzu: podľa Davida sa scéna hrala vo viacerých variantoch (Childs-Vec / MacReady-Vec / ani jeden) → herecký prejav vo výslednom strihu môže pochádzať z rôznych „interpretačných“ záberov.
- **SYFY Wire, orálna história 2022** [S03] — **overené v origináli**:
  > (o fľaši/benzíne) "We were both drinking out of the same bottle, so why would that be gasoline and [why would] he not be as dead as I would be? ...   > (o dychu) "I don't know the last time that you were in the cold and standing by a fire [but] if you're in the cold, more than likely the wind is blowing. ... If the wind is blowing in my direction, and the heat from the fire is coming at me, you're not gonna see the smoke come out of my mouth the same way as if someone on the other side of the fire ..."
  - Pozn.: podľa Roba Agera (2017) MacReady v záverečnej scéne z fľaše viditeľne neodpije, len ju pred Childsovým príchodom dvíha k tvári; fľaša je už otvorená a aspoň do polovice prázdna. Davidovo „both drinking out of the same bottle“ je teda skôr spomienka/argument než popis záberu — odporúčam overiť v obraze.
- **Dread Central, 14. 12. 2021** (rozhovor k *Creepshow* S2) [S06] — **overené v origináli**: na otázku „what was your interpretation of the ending of The Thing?“ odpoveď: *"KD: It wasn't me."* (titulok článku: „Childs Definitely Wasn't The Thing“ — titulok preháňa krátky žartovný výrok).
- **Hollywood Walk of Fame, ceremónia Johna Carpentera, 3. 4. 2025** [S07]: úvod reči Keitha Davida *"First, let me clear this up – it wasn't me."* — podľa Fangoria (reportáž z miesta) a ComicBook.com (4. 4. 2025); stav overenia pozri tabuľku (video MaximoTV na YouTube CTm1Fr5U7K0).
- **Hodnotenie:** David konzistentne žartovne popiera, že by bol Vec, ale v jedinom dlhšom vecnom vyjadrení (Guardian 2024) hovorí, že sa hrali všetky tri varianty a že „The Thing is whoever you think it is“. Nie je to autoritatívne potvrdenie.

### 3.2 Kurt Russell
- **Happy Sad Confused (Josh Horowitz), živé nahrávanie v 92NY, YouTube 14. 12. 2023 (id 71GokYdUIYU), kapitola „Insights on 'The Thing'“, 20:41–21:44** [S08] — **overené v origináli (video, strojový prepis)**. SYFY Wire (19. 12. 2023) to uvádza ako „MTV's Josh Horowitz“. Otázka: "Do you know what he has in mind? Do you know what the answer is about which of them is?" Russell:
  > "We talked about the ending of that movie, John and I, for a long, long time. We kind of like trade ideas for the end, write it out. And it was one of those things where John was concerned about it, doing a movie that you would see for two hours plus and bring you back to square one. And we finally got to a point where we tried different things. And I just remember finally saying, how about this one? And he said [?], John, I think this comes back to square one. I think that's what it does. And the only thing I could do was finish it with, why don't we just sit here for a while and see what happens."
  - Russell **na otázku, či pozná odpoveď, priamo neodpovedá**. SYFY citát mierne uhladzuje (vynecháva „how about this one?“).
  - Vedľajší fakt k dychu: Russell spomína, že exteriérový set v Stewart sa držal na 28 °F, lebo sa na konci vyhadzoval do vzduchu a mal byť zasnežený.

## 4. Ostatný štáb — Ramsay, Bottin, Lancaster, Cohen, Morricone; alternatívne konce, scenár

### 4.1 Stuart Cohen (koproducent) — blog „The Original Fan“ (2011–2013) [S14]
Primárny a najpodrobnejší zdroj o vzniku konca. Blog: theoriginalfan.blogspot.com („A Producer's Guide to the Evolution and Production of John Carpenter's The Thing“). **Overené v origináli** (texty stiahnuté cez Blogger feed a prečítané).

**„THE ENDING YOU ALMOST SAW“ (2. 10. 2011)** — kľúčové body (citáty doslovne):
- "There were three endings shot and / or edited for THE THING. They are: (1) The ending as it now exists, little changed from Bill Lancaster's first draft... (2) McCready awaiting a final blood test at McMurdo Station. ... (3) Childs disappearing forever into the snowy night, leaving McCready alone."
- K verzii (2): "This was actually one long shot filmed at Rob's special effects facility, Heartland, on a day sometime after principal photography when Kurt was available ... a very deliberate camera dolly down a deserted industrial hallway, slowing panning left and eventually coming to a stop at an open doorway to reveal McCready alive, alone, and shivering seated on a gurney at the far end of the room. I think he was dressed as he was in the final scene, with the same blanket wrapped around his shoulders. No Childs, and no other actors or extras." / "My impression at the time is that this was an honest but half-hearted attempt to provide closure ( and clarity ) ... and John was never serious about using it." / "Never tested or screened, I last saw this piece of film sitting off by itself at the end of an editing room bench..."
  - **Pozor (rozpor):** Cohen píše „awaiting a final blood test“; strihač Todd Ramsay (1998, S29) naopak „he's just had a blood test so that we know that he's not infected“. Médiá (Looper, /Film, Gizmodo 2013 „proven to be human“) preberajú Ramsayho verziu. Záber nebol zverejnený, rozpor sa nedá rozhodnúť.
- Prvá predpremiéra: "late May 1982 at the Red Rock theatre in Las Vegas", druhá nasledujúcu noc v Denveri. "But the largest block of criticism was reserved for the ambiguity inherent in the film ... Who was who ? ... does anybody in this movie win ?"
- Strihové pokusy: "A version was tried that eliminated most of the early part of the scene ( Childs' evasive answers to McCready's questions ), in an attempt to make him seem less suspicious. Alternate takes were parsed for more affirmative line readings - with John's permission, Universal's Verna Fields headed to the editing room ..."
- Verzia (3) bez Childsa vyhrala test na pozemku Universalu o "three or four percent" a bola schválená na uvedenie; Cohen a Helena Hacker presvedčili Neda Tanena a "he gave his approval to restore the original ending, some 10 hours before release printing was to begin. As a last request, we added the scream heard over the exterior of the final camp explosion to resolve the monsters death for those 'who choose to believe it' as Tannen put it."
- O identite: "Most of you want to know who was who in the final scene. Unsurprisingly, there is no definitive answer but I can tell you what Bill Lancaster thought when he wrote it - he believed both men were human. ... I subscribe to this view. I can not, however with 100% certainty tell you that John agrees with this - what I can say is that given the fact this scene still bears the remnants of this massive re-working with many hands it is counterintuitive for me to think that there exists a hidden level of meaning that offers clues to identity (The J&B bottle being passed back and forth, Childs lack of breath in several cuts, ect. ). Believe me, all we were trying to do was keep the scene in the movie..."
- **Dôsledok pre forenznú analýzu:** záverečná scéna je podľa producenta výsledkom opakovaného prestrihávania (vrátane výberu „afirmatívnejších“ záberov a skracovania Childsových vyhýbavých odpovedí). Detaily kontinuity v nej (dych, fľaša) považuje Cohen skôr za náhodné. To je v napätí s Carpenterovými výrokmi 2023–2026 o „indícii“.

**„THE SCREENPLAY“ (13. 2. 2012):** Lancaster o poslednej scéne: "It was here we discussed the last scene ( his feeling: human, but written with plenty of room for argument )." Štúdio pri scenári "expressed no concern over the ambiguity of the ending, later to be the cause of so much angst."

**„THE MUSIC“ (27. 9. 2011):** Morricone dodal hudbu ako tematické suity bez klasického „spottingu“: "Morricone would provide the music in a series of thematic suites, with both electronic and orchestral elements present, that we would then later edit to fit ... eliminate the need to conventionally 'spot' the film." / "there was still the need for more specific transition and suspense cues which John, along with his partner, Alan Howarth, then supplied." → hudba v záverečnej scéne nebola komponovaná na konkrétny výklad konca. Žiadne vyjadrenie Morriconeho k záveru som nenašiel.

### 4.2 Stuart Cohen — Q&A pre Outpost #31 (fórum, cca 2012–2013; kompilácia .docx z 2018) [S15]
**Overené v origináli** (dokument na outpost31.com). Doslovne:
- Otázka: "Was there any actual difference between the coat that Childs wore throughout the movie and the one at the end, or were they the same coat?" — Cohen: "I think it's the same coat. I sent Rob Ager a fan letter when I first saw his analysis which I thought was thoroughgoing and scholarly, but I don't believe there was anything specifically planned by John to indicate identity at the end. The scene was re-worked so many different times in editing in order to save it from being entirely cut that anything that arises ( Child's lack of breath in two cuts, for instance ) was probably accidental…"
- "Was there a definite idea whether Childs or Mac was infected at the end?" — "No definite idea"
- "How many different endings were discussed?" — "Discussed with John and Bill, only the one – three were shot and or edited…"
- K psovi utekajúcemu z tábora (TV verzia): "Forget about the fake alternate ending…"
- "I said it was Bill Lancaster who felt that way in conversation with me. I specifically said in the blog John has kept his own council on the matter. ... But by stating 'all the rumors are untrue' regarding the ending John has put to bed the idea that he planted any clues to identity throughout the film ( Child's breath, the coat. the whiskey bottle, etc.)..."
- Ostatné „otvorené“ momenty sú podľa Cohena zámerne nedefinované aj pre tvorcov (Fuchs: "Neither John nor I have a clue as to who ran by Fuchs in the hallway – it was designed that way"; kto pripravil roztrhané šortky: "Left open ended").

### 4.3 Bill Lancaster — scenár [S16]
- **„Second Draft, March 4, 1981“** (prepis na IMSDb) — **overené v origináli (text scenára, nie skenovaný originál)**. Záver sa odohráva v „pub area“ s šachovnicou; kľúčové pokyny: "Both men speak guardedly and stare at each other suspiciously." Childs: "If you're worried about anything, let's take that blood test of yours." MacReady: "If we've got any surprises for each other -- we shouldn't be in any condition to do anything about it. (beat) You play chess?" ... "MacReady grins and hands the bottle to Childs. Childs smiles back and takes a healthy swig." Fľaša je whisky z baru, z ktorej MacReady skôr sám pije ("You like whiskey? ... He takes a drink"; "He pours himself a drink"). Scenár nerieši, kto je Vec.

### 4.4 Todd Ramsay (strihač)
- Podľa Cohena (SYFY Wire 2022) bol záber zachráneného MacReadyho natočený na Ramsayov návrh: "So John, to assuage his editor, made one shot. He wasn't thrilled about doing it. It wasn't all that great a shot, but he made it." ... "It would have been a long, slow lap dissolve from the scene you now see, you'd hear the sounds of a helicopter rescue or something." — **overené v origináli (SYFY Wire)**, ale je to Cohenova výpoveď, nie Ramsayova.
- **Vlastné Ramsayove slová — dokument *Terror Takes Shape* (r. Michael Matessino, 1998, bonus na DVD/Blu-ray)** [S29] — **overené v origináli** (YouTube nahrávka „The Thing (1982) | 'Terror Takes Shape' Documentary“, kanál DVDXtras, 28. 6. 2024, id FMD9kiNVh_k, čas ~72:13–73:58; hovoriaci identifikovaný porovnaním s úvodnou titulkou „TODD RAMSAY Film Editor“ na 1:51; strojový prepis):
  > "The reviews were mixed, I think, with the audience. There was an alternate ending to the film that was shot. I had spoken to John about this. I had said that I felt, well, gee, this is a terribly nihilistic ending. It's very much of a downbeat. Perhaps we should protect ourselves while we have the principal, Kurt, and just shoot an ending where we see that he survives. And John was open to this. So essentially what he did was he staged a scene in which Kurt has been rescued and is sitting in a kind of small office structure. And he's just had a blood test so that we know that he's not infected. And he has survived this ordeal rather than the kind of mutual death that concludes the film now. He just shot this for protection. We never previewed this. The issue did come up at the conclusion of the two previews as to whether we should perhaps try this ending, see how it flew with the audiences, and or make other adjustments on the film. And I think John essentially at that time felt that he had achieved in the movie what he wanted to do. ... And so that's the way the film went out."
  - **Rozpor medzi očitými svedkami:** Ramsay (1998): MacReady „has just had a blood test so that we know that he's not infected“; Cohen (2011): „awaiting a final blood test“, „No Childs“. Obaja sa zhodujú: jeden záber, natočený „for protection“ po hlavnom natáčaní, nikdy nepremietnutý. **Ramsay je teda zdrojom populárneho tvrdenia „krvný test dokazuje, že MacReady je človek“** — nejde o výmysel médií, ale o jednu z dvoch protichodných spomienok. Záber nebol zverejnený (SYFY Wire 19. 12. 2023 píše „has since been lost“ — bez zdroja, neoverené).
  - Ramsay hovorí o „the two previews“ — zhodne s Cohenom (Las Vegas, Denver); o neskoršom teste verzie bez Childsa na pozemku Universalu (Cohen) sa v tejto pasáži nezmieňa.

## 5. Fanúšikovské teórie k záverečnej scéne a ich vyvrátenia

Pozn.: „autor/popularizátor“ = najstarší zdroj, ktorý som vedel doložiť; skutočný prvý výskyt na fórach (Outpost #31, IMDb boards) z 90. a 2000. rokov sa väčšinou nedá spoľahlivo datovať.

### (a) Childs nemá viditeľný dych
- **Pôvod/šírenie:** fórový folklór, existoval už pred 2011 (Cohen na blogu 2. 10. 2011 ho spomína ako známy: "Childs lack of breath in several cuts"). Masovo rozšírený fámou z Redditu (kleinbl00, február 2013, cez Giant Freakin Robot → io9/Gizmodo 19. 2. 2013 a CinemaBlend 20. 2. 2013), podľa ktorej mal Carpenter povedať, že je "glaringly obvious that Kurt Russell is breathing and Keith David is not" — **neoverené, tretia ruka**. Aj Blumhouse (2016) a WhatCulture (10. 4. 2018) ho uvádzajú ako hlavný argument.
- **Vyvrátenia (najsilnejšie):**
  1. **Stuart Cohen (producent):** dych chýba len "in two cuts" / "in several cuts" a je "probably accidental" — scéna bola mnohokrát prestrihávaná (S14, S15).
  2. **Keith David:** pri ohni a vetre zo strany ohňa sa para nevytvára/nie je vidieť (SYFY 2022; Guardian 2024: "if I'm downstage of the fire you wouldn't see steam coming from my mouth because there's too much heat").
  3. **Rob Ager (Collative Learning, video „THE THING 'No breath' theory examined“, YouTube 21. 4. 2017)** — hoci sám tvrdí, že Childs je Vec: Childsov dych je "actually visible in places, but you have to pay attention for it"; **Bennings-Vec v noci vonku má "full on breath condensation"**; dokonalá imitácia musí mať pľúca, reč vyžaduje výdych. Na svojom webe: "no I don't theorize that his lack of visible breath makes him inhuman. The breath thing is just a light and shadow issue." (**overené v origináli** — video prepísané, web prečítaný).
  4. /Film (Eric Vespe, 19. 1. 2023) a Dread Central (Padraig Cotter, 14. 9. 2016): rovnaký argument s Benningsom; Dread Central tvrdí, že Childsov dych je v scéne viditeľný.
  5. **Výrobné okolnosti (SYFY Wire 2022, S03):** para z úst bola na pľaci problém: na štúdiu sa stratila, keď sa zohriali svetlá (Larry Franco: "before we broke for lunch, we had no breath anymore"), na ľadovci v Stewart (B.C.) bol vzduch príliš suchý (Clyde Bryan: "there was a lot of drinking of hot beverages and stuff to make breath on some of the scenes"). Keith David: záverečná scéna sa točila v noci po výbuchoch tábora, "winding down to the last day".
  6. Carpenter (2023) teóriu s dychom výslovne nekomentoval; o fámach z 2013 pozri 1.6.

### (b) Fľaša = benzín (Molotov), Childs nereaguje
- **Pôvod:** anonymný fórový/YouTube folklór; Rob Ager ju v 2017 nazýva „popular 'molotov whisky' interpretation“ a uvádza, že ju v komentároch pod jeho videom presadzovali diváci. WhatCulture (2018) ju podáva ako „final nail in the coffin“ („the established drinker never takes a sip“, MacReadyho „chuckle“).
- **Fakty z filmu/scenára, o ktoré sa opiera:** vo filme MacReady a Nauls nesú do generátorovne Molotovove koktaily vo fľašiach rovnakej značky/tvaru ako MacReadyho whisky (Ager, 2017); v scenári 1981 Sanchez plní prázdne fľaše benzínom.
- **Vyvrátenia:**
  1. **Rob Ager, „THE THING movie 'Molotov whisky' theory examined“ (YouTube 26. 4. 2017)** — **overené v origináli**: MacReadyho Molotovy zničí Blair-Vec v generátorovni; MacReady potom robí kotúľ — nemôže mať fľaše v bunde; fľaša je už otvorená a aspoň do polovice prázdna; MacReady ju dvíha k ústam ešte predtým, než vie o Childsovi; podľa scenára si nalieva a zapaľuje cigaru; Vec imituje dokonale (aj chuťové poháriky) — "there probably isn't a single organic animal on earth that could drink gasoline and not know it."
  2. **Keith David (SYFY 2022):** "We were both drinking out of the same bottle ... [Mac] really is not Houdini, so where would he have switched the bottles?"
  3. **Stuart Cohen:** "The J&B bottle being passed back and forth" medzi príkladmi, kde neverí v skryté indície (S14, S15).
  4. **Scenár (1981):** fľaša je whisky z baru, MacReady z nej pije (S16).
  5. **Carpenter (SYFY 2022):** teórie typu "Well, he took a drink, so he must be contaminated" označil za "hilarious" — formulácia skôr naráža na variant „Childs pil = nakazený“.
- **Variant 2025 (Joe Russo, X 23. 3. 2025; GamesRadar 24. 3. 2025):** nie benzín, ale porušenie pravidla „jedzte/pite len vlastné“ — keď MacReady podá fľašu a Childs pije, „The Thing has won“. Interpretácia fanúšika, nie výrok tvorcov.

### (c) Náušnica u Childsa
- **Pôvod:** odvodené z prequelu *The Thing* (2011, r. Matthijs van Heijningen Jr.), kde Vec nevie napodobniť anorganický materiál (plomby, náušnica). WhatCulture (10. 4. 2018) to podáva ako argument pre Childsovu ľudskosť, hneď s protiargumentom, že Vec sa mohla naučiť nosiť šperky obete.
- **Vyvrátenie/obmedzenie:** pravidlo neexistuje v scenári ani vo filme z 1982 (v scenári 1981 ani v Cohenových textoch nie je o anorganickej hmote nič) → ide o retroaktívne pravidlo iného filmu. Či Childs v záverečnej scéne náušnicu má, si musí overiť analytik z obrazu — žiadny zdroj od tvorcov to nekomentuje. Autora teórie sa mi nepodarilo dohľadať (Reddit/fóra po 2011).

### (d) Childsova bunda/parka mení farbu alebo typ
- **Pôvod:** **Rob Ager, dvojdielna video-analýza (2011)** — Gizmodo/io9 o nej píše 21. 8. 2011. Tvrdenie: Childs mal naposledy tmavomodrú bundu, na konci sa zdá béžová; Vec trhá oblečenie obetí.
- **Vyvrátenia:**
  1. **Sám Rob Ager, „THE THING movie 'Clothing continuity' theory examined“ (YouTube 17. 5. 2017)** — **overené v origináli**: na Blu-ray zistil, že "the child's coat in the ending is in fact a dark coat that's finally [finely] covered in frost, and it was actually the orange firelight that made it look beige" — teda vlastnú teóriu farby čiastočne odvolal. (Novú teóriu však ponúka: v zábere po Childsovom zmiznutí sa na vešiakoch prehodia bundy a chýba tmavomodrá bunda „near enough identical to Child's coat“ → Childs-Vec si mohol obliecť náhradnú.)
  2. **Stuart Cohen (Outpost #31 Q&A):** "I think it's the same coat ... I don't believe there was anything specifically planned by John to indicate identity at the end."
  3. **BAMF Style (15. 1. 2023, kostýmová analýza):** Childs má po celý film jednu modrú nylonovú parku s kožušinou na kapucni; "Childs dresses exclusively in blue" (zdroj nekomentuje záver — len dokladá, že kostým je jeden).
- Pre forenznú analýzu: farbu treba posudzovať s ohľadom na oranžové osvetlenie ohňom a námrazu; Agerova novšia „teória vešiakov“ je nezávislá a overiteľná z obrazu.

### (e) Odlesk v očiach (catchlight)
- **Pôvod:** Dean Cundey, komentár Blu-ray Scream Factory 2016 — **len pre Palmera v scéne krvného testu**. Na záver to rozšíril moderátor Rob Galluzzo (Blumhouse, 29. 8. 2016): "Kurt Russell has the gleam lighting in his eyes. Childs does not!"
- **Vyvrátenia:** **Rob Galluzzo sám (YouTube 15. 10. 2024, S28): "it was, in fact, just me being silly. Apologies to Dean!!!!"**; Cundey sám (záver zámerne nevyriešený, S05/S28, S03); Clyde Bryan ("on the ending, I don't remember any conversations about the light in people's eyes"); Carpenter 2023 ("He has no clue ... we were in the snow"); **Rob Ager, „THE THING - 'Eye gleam' theory examined“ (YouTube 17. 4. 2017)**: Palmer má odlesky v iných záberoch tej istej scény, odlesky majú aj Norris-Vec, Blair-imitácia a psy-imitácie → "the theory doesn't hold up at all when you look at the entire movie"; Dread Central 2016: svetlo sa v Childsových očiach objavuje viackrát.

### (f) MacReadyho úsmev / smiech
- **Výklady:** (1) „Molotov“ — smeje sa, lebo Childs vypil benzín (WhatCulture 2018: "MacReady's world-weary chuckle"); (2) Ager 2017 — "he's acknowledging Childs' lack of fear about being infected by sharing a bottle that Mac had already drunk from" (Ager pritom vkladá klip skoršej scény; z audioprepisu nie je jasné ktorej); (3) Russo 2025 — „Thing has won“.
- **Tvorcovia:** v scenári (1981) je pokyn "MacReady grins and hands the bottle to Childs. Childs smiles back and takes a healthy swig." Cohen uvádza, že pri prestrihávaní sa hľadali "more affirmative line readings" (S14). Žiadny výrok tvorcov o význame smiechu som nenašiel.

### (g) Ďalšie časté teórie
- **MacReady je Vec:** /Film (Eric Vespe, 19. 1. 2023) — MacReady pil z Blairovej fľaše, bol posledný u Fuchsa, jeho test krvi nevidíme odobrať, plameňomet „zlyhá“. **Komentár 1998** (S09): tvorcovia sami pripúšťajú „He could have been ... we didn't see him for a while“. Carpenter 2025: herci nevedeli.
- **Ani jeden nie je Vec (obaja zmrznú):** Bill Lancaster (podľa Cohena), Stuart Cohen, David Clennon (SYFY 2022: "my personal take on it is that neither of them is the Thing and they're gonna die in the ruins of that outpost").
- **Childs zmizol podozrivo / Blair-Vec ho napadol pri dverách:** Ager 2017 (scéna s otvorenými dverami, generátor vypnutý hneď po Childsovom odchode). Childs vo filme: "I thought I saw Blair..." V komentári 1998: "That's where he explained what he was doing, looking for Blair."
- **Vec zahynula vo výbuchu:** výkrik pri poslednej explózii pridaný na žiadosť Neda Tanena pre tých, "who choose to believe it" (Cohen, S14) — teda zámerne nejednoznačný zvukový signál.
- **„Indícia v strede filmu“ (2025/2026):** od Carpentera (S13, S01) — obsah neznámy; všetky „riešenia“ (Russo 2025 a i.) sú fanúšikovské.

## 6. Kino vs. TV verzia

- **Kino (1982):** končí dialógom MacReady–Childs, podaním fľaše, MacReadyho smiechom a celkom horiaceho tábora; podľa Cohena bol na poslednú chvíľu (pri obnovení pôvodného konca) pridaný **výkrik Veci počas exteriéru poslednej explózie**, aby si divák mohol myslieť, že monštrum zomrelo (S14).
- **TV verzia (premiéra CBS 1986; neskôr syndikácia):** podľa Cohena "prepared without our knowledge, consultation, or approval" ... "This version is really his work" (Sid Sheinberg, prezident Universalu); Cohen ju volá "the MSTK3000 version" a pes na konci je "the fake alternate ending" (S15). Carpenter ju podľa Cohena opísal ako verziu, kde "Sid Sheinberg tried to teach me how to make a horror movie" (Carpenterov pôvodný výrok som nedohľadal).
- **Overené vlastným pozretím** (YouTube klip „The Thing - Character Introductions & Alternate Ending“, digdugsmug, 10. 11. 2013, id S0euB529UHA; záznam vysielania stanice Bounce TV, TV-14) [S17]:
  - rozprávač predstavuje postavy pri prvom výskyte (napr. "MacReady, a top helicopter pilot, worked for Hughes Aircraft as a test pilot until he got into a confrontation with top management and resigned..."; Childs: "a mechanic who went from trade school to the airlines, none better");
  - koniec: nočný celok horiaceho tábora → **denné** zábery ľadovca, dym nad táborom, **pes (husky) beží po snehu, zastaví sa, obzrie sa a odchádza** → rozprávač: *"Who knows what has come from the galaxy? Who knows what evil lurks in the sky beyond [?] ... Watch those around you. Who knows what today, tonight, or tomorrow will bring?"* (strojový prepis; „beyond God“ je pravdepodobne chybne počuté).
  - Záver: TV verzia pridáva **jednoznačný signál, že Vec prežila (pes)**, čo v kinoverzii nie je. Pre otázku Childs/MacReady nič nerieši a nie je autorská.
- Ďalšie rozdiely (Offscreen, Douglas Buck, 5. 7. 2018; Cracked 13. 6. 2021): cca 12 min vystrihnutých, pan&scan 1.33:1, dabované vulgarizmy, vystrihnuté efekty (Norris), rozprávačova kóda na konci. — **overené zo sekundárneho zdroja**.
- Pozn.: Cohen uvádza, že vystrihnuté scény na DVD sú v 1.33:1, lebo boli preformátované práve pre TV verziu.

## Najčastejšie omyly a nedohľadateľné citáty

1. **„Dean Cundey prezradil, že Childs je Vec (nemá odlesk v očiach).“** — NEPRAVDA. Cundey hovoril len o Palmerovi v krvnom teste; o závere výslovne povedal, že Carpenter chcel otázku nechať otvorenú („if one, or both, or neither of these guys is 'the thing'“, 2016; „John never would reveal the answer or maybe never had an answer“, 2022). Rozšírenie na Childsa je od moderátora Roba Galluzza (Blumhouse 2016) a médií — a Galluzzo ho v roku 2024 sám označil za žart („just me being silly. Apologies to Dean!!!!“). Carpenter (2023) teóriu odmietol.
2. **„Carpenter na komentári (1998) naznačil, kto je Vec.“** — Nedoložené; v komentári hovorí „We just didn't know“, pripúšťa aj MacReadyho („He could have been“ — neisté, kto z dvojice to hovorí) a koniec nazýva „ambiguous“.
3. **„Carpenter vždy tvrdil, že koniec nemá riešenie.“** — Neplatí pre obdobie po 2022: od 2022 hovorí, že vie (SYFY), od októbra 2023 (Colbert), že sa to dá zistiť pozorným sledovaním („Yes, you can.“), 2025 „a giant hint in the middle of the film“, 2026 „It's right in the movie too. There is a big clue.“ V 1998 však hovoril „we just didn't know“ a producent Cohen (2011–13) tvrdí, že nebola „definite idea“ a že Carpenter vyhlásil „all the rumors are untrue“. Tvrdenia sa v čase posunuli; Carpenter ich podáva žartovne (šek poštou, „sworn to secrecy“).
4. **„Carpenter povedal, že je zjavné, že Keith David nedýcha.“** (Reddit 2013) — **nedohľadateľné, tretia ruka** (kamarát Redditora); io9 to samo označilo za „completely unverified rumor“. Rob Ager, hoci zástanca „Childs je Vec“, dychovú teóriu odmieta.
5. **„Alternatívny koniec: MacReady je zachránený a krvný test dokáže, že je človek.“** — Čiastočne. Záber existoval (jeden záber MacReadyho po záchrane, bez Childsa, natočený „for protection“ na podnet strihača Todda Ramsayho, nikdy nepremietnutý). Spomienky sa však líšia: Ramsay (1998) „he's just had a blood test so that we know that he's not infected“, Cohen (2011) „awaiting a final blood test“ a podľa neho s ním Carpenter vážne nepočítal. Médiá (Looper, /Film) prebrali Ramsayho verziu ako fakt bez upozornenia na rozpor.
6. **„Štúdio testovalo koniec s helikoptérou / psom.“** — Pes na konci je z TV verzie (1986), ktorú autori nepripravovali („fake alternate ending“ — Cohen). Testovaná alternatíva bola verzia bez Childsa (MacReady sám) — vyhrala test o 3–4 %, bola schválená a ~10 hodín pred tlačou kópií zrušená.
7. **„Keith David potvrdil, že Childs nie je Vec.“** — Jeho „It wasn't me“ (2021, 2025) je žart; vo vecnom vyjadrení (Guardian 2024) hovorí, že sa hrali tri varianty a „The Thing is whoever you think it is“. Carpenter (2025): „They had no clue ... Actors don't know anything.“
8. **„Childsova bunda je na konci béžová.“** — Autor teórie Rob Ager ju v 2017 sám čiastočne odvolal (tmavá bunda pokrytá námrazou v oranžovom svetle ohňa); Cohen: „I think it's the same coat.“
9. **„Pravidlo náušnice/anorganickej hmoty platí pre film z 1982.“** — Pochádza z prequelu 2011; v scenári ani vo filme 1982 nie je.
10. **„Indícia v strede filmu = fľaša/zákaz zdieľať jedlo.“** — To je výklad fanúšika Joea Russa (2025), ktorý médiá (GamesRadar) podali ako „vyriešené“. Carpenter obsah indície neuviedol.
11. **Lancasterov pôvodný koniec** — protichodné tvrdenia: Carpenter (1999) „both MacReady and Charles turning into the Thing and being rescued in the spring“ vs. Cohen (2011): výsledný koniec „little changed from Bill Lancaster's first draft“ a Lancaster považoval oboch za ľudí. Bez prístupu k prvému draftu nerozhodnuteľné; „Second Draft“ (4. 3. 1981) sa s filmom zhoduje.
12. **Nedohľadané pôvodné výroky:** (a) Carpenterovo „all the rumors are untrue“ (cituje ho len Cohen); (b) Carpenterov opis TV verzie „Sid Sheinberg tried to teach me how to make a horror movie“ (cituje Cohen); (c) údajný Carpenterov výrok z 2013 „Now, I do know, in the end, who the Thing is, but I cannot tell you“ (uvádza ho len súhrn vyhľadávača; v dostupných článkoch z 2013 som ho nenašiel — overené ekvivalenty sú až z 2022/2023); (e) akékoľvek vyjadrenie Roba Bottina alebo Ennia Morriconeho k identite na konci — nenašiel som žiadne.
